Essential Pet Care Guide

Select a pet category below to view tailored nutrition, habitat, and wellness tips.

🍖 Nutrition & Hydration

Feed high-quality age-appropriate dog food twice daily and keep fresh, clean water available at all times. Avoid chocolate, grapes, and onions.

🏃 Exercise & Play

Provide 30–60 minutes of daily walks, fetch, or mental stimulation toys to prevent boredom and maintain a healthy weight.

🛁 Grooming & Hygiene

Brush their coat weekly, trim nails once a month, and brush teeth regularly using pet-safe toothpaste.

🩺 Vet & Vaccinations

Schedule annual wellness checkups, keep rabies and booster shots up to date, and maintain monthly flea and tick prevention.

🐟 Nutrition & Hydration

Offer balanced wet and dry cat food rich in animal protein. Place water bowls away from food dishes to encourage drinking.

🧶 Enrichment & Scratching

Provide sturdy scratching posts, window perches, and interactive wand toys for 15–20 minutes of daily play.

🧼 Litter Box Care

Scoop the litter box daily and wash it with mild soap every two weeks. Keep it in a quiet, easily accessible spot.

🩺 Vet & Indoor Safety

Keep cats safely indoors, schedule yearly vet exams, and ensure houseplants like lilies are kept out of reach.

🌾 Balanced Diet

Combine formulated pellets with fresh leafy greens, chopped fruits, and occasional seeds. Change water twice daily.

🏡 Cage & Environment

Choose a spacious cage with natural wooden perches of varying sizes, placed away from drafts and kitchen fumes.

🎶 Social Interaction

Spend daily out-of-cage supervised time talking, whistling, and offering foraging toys to keep birds mentally stimulated.

🩺 Avian Health

Provide shallow water dishes for bathing and schedule yearly checkups with an avian veterinarian.

🥬 Hay & Greens

Unlimited Timothy hay should make up 80% of a rabbit's diet, supplemented with fresh romaine lettuce, herbs, and limited pellets.

🏠 Safe Living Space

House rabbits indoors in a large exercise pen or bunny-proofed room with soft flooring and hideaway boxes.

🪮 Gentle Grooming

Brush weekly (more during shedding seasons) to prevent hairballs. Never bathe a rabbit in water as it causes severe stress.

🩺 Dental & Vet Care

Chewing hay naturally wears down continuously growing teeth. Visit an exotic-pet vet annually for checkups.
